# Screenshot gallery

These unretouched screenshots show the actual browser game at a 430 × 932 CSS-pixel phone viewport, captured at 2× scale. They are not photographs of a physical Android device.

- `library.png`: the bundled ten-song library.
- `dachshund-hold.png`: a held dog nearing its tail, with a live combo and particles.
- `slide-ribbon.png`: an active slide and combo flames.
- `practice-results.png`: the results of a short Expert practice run.

Gameplay uses the included Monkeys Spinning Monkeys recording and its generated Expert arrangement, with scripted two-pointer input through the browser's actual gameplay handlers. Practice is unranked. Screenshots contain no imported music or personal leaderboard entries.
