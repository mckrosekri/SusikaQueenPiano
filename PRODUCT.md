# SusikaQueenPiano

## Register
product

## Users
Casual and experienced Android music-game players using a phone in portrait, playing with two thumbs.

## Product Purpose
Make playing along with a song fun, immediate and musical. Four lanes, reliable audio timing, meaningful difficulty scaling and private local music imports.

## Brand Personality
Immersive, playful, electric. The experience feels like playing a flowing piano track.

## Design Principles
- Large readable piano tiles can be pressed anywhere while visible.
- Difficulty adds musical detail and coordination without impossible gestures.
- Reward tile completion and streaks; precise timing is not required.
- Every interaction works offline on Android; imported audio stays private.
- Spectacle rewards the music without obscuring touch targets.

## Anti-references
Cluttered neon dashboards, generic card walls, constant flashing, advertisement interruptions and fake song analysis.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Large mobile controls, keyboard menu navigation, reduced motion, non-color note cues, adjustable effects and calibration. Gameplay supports four keyboard keys as a desktop alternative.
