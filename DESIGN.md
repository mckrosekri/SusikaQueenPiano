# SusikaQueenPiano direction

Four lanes in portrait, always at most two fingers. A personal piano world for Susika: blush pink, lilac, cream, dark plum text and rose accents. Menus have crisp type, calm spacing and clear controls. Gameplay occupies the full phone width.

The four piano lanes use pastel colors with contrasting plum tap tiles. Plain tiles have uninterrupted satin surfaces and a quiet pearl rim, with no center marks or floating highlight bars. Direction chevrons appear only on flick and slide gestures. Hold tiles are long dachshunds with soft sausage bodies, floppy ears, bows and dangling legs; their body length represents the authored hold duration. Slides retain a distinct ribbon and an uncluttered satin head. The entire visible tile is a touch target. A glowing cream line marks the musical beat: the middle of each tap/flick reaches it on its authored attack. A forgiving band around the line earns full points; distance outside it smoothly lowers credit. Successful early/late taps keep the combo. Sustain heads reach the line at the attack and their tails arrive at the musical end; full-duration contact earns full credit. Effects are bounded; no rapid full-screen strobing. Gesture geometry distinguishes tap/hold/flick/ribbon tiles. Easter eggs are gentle and optional; they never alter scoring or difficulty.

Menu motion: 150–250ms state transitions. Game feedback is immediate. Stage effects sit behind notes and respond to hits and musical energy. Reduced motion removes decorative motion while preserving note travel.

Gameplay motion: static satin lane shading and narrow cream rails frame the four lanes. A hit lights its lane, releases a short translucent tile echo, and emits a compact fan of hearts, stars or diamonds. Held dogs keep a full-size face at the eating edge, with lightly swinging paws; connected fingers emit independent star streams. A completed hold or slide gets a stronger local reward. Effects are pooled and bounded. Minimal effects and OS reduced motion use fading accents; full effects enable decorative movement. Pixi updates geometry before rendering from the audio clock, and resume holds the paused frame through a visible count-in.

Evidence belongs in output/playwright and output/android; generated binaries belong in output/android. Logo and icon use the global Pixel2Motion workflow.

Combo tile fire uses a narrow feathered warm rim with three cached tapered rose/amber/cream tongue shapes. Tongues rise, curl and dissolve at independent seeded phases. Fire sits above note art along the rim, below hit feedback; horizontal bounds stay inside the canvas on both outer lanes.
